Business and support

This website and SMS program are operated by Nicolas Roberto Muggli, operating as Digital Consulting USA. For privacy questions or requests, contact team@digitalconsultingusa.com or +1 (651) 615-7171. Our business voice number and intended SMS sender is +1 (651) 615-7171. Text messaging remains paused pending registration, approval, number association, and operational verification.

Information we collect and use

When you submit a contact or contractor application form, we collect your name, company, email, phone, website, trade, service area, service radius, project value, budget, start timeframe, goals, and other answers you provide. We use this information to respond, assess service fit, schedule appointments, provide agreed services, maintain business records, and protect our systems. Do not send sensitive personal information or homeowner records.

Forms pass through a Cloudflare Worker to GoHighLevel for contact and opportunity management and email follow-up. Server records include the receipt time, submission reference, source URL, language, attribution, and a disclosure version. Contact and application submissions record no SMS consent. The chat widget is the only current SMS opt-in surface. A phone number alone is never SMS consent. Text messaging is currently paused.

Cookies and analytics

Display and language preferences are stored in your browser. Campaign parameters and advertising click IDs may be kept for the current tab to preserve inquiry attribution and booking context. Choosing Essential Only clears that attribution. GA4 analytics stays off until you accept optional analytics. Change your choice through Privacy Settings in the footer. Form answers, names, emails, and phone numbers are not sent to website analytics.

Service providers and external links

Services used by this website include GitHub Pages hosting, Cloudflare form processing, GoHighLevel CRM, Google Fonts, optional Google Analytics, Cal.com scheduling, a WhatsApp contact link, a GoHighLevel chat widget, and an externally hosted Sara voice widget. These services may process technical information such as IP address, browser, device, requested pages, and access times. External tools process information under their own policies when loaded or used. Messages and contact details you choose to submit through the chat widget are processed by GoHighLevel to respond to your inquiry. Providing a phone number in chat alone does not authorize SMS. Voice or conversation information may be sent to the voice widget operator; avoid sensitive information.

After a confirmed form submission, a booking link may include your name and email to prefill Cal.com. Cal.com booking events are processed through the server to update CRM appointments. The site tracks booking clicks separately from confirmed bookings, subject to analytics consent.

Mobile information and SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

This restriction controls over other sharing language in this policy. Consent evidence is processed in our secured receipt storage and CRM by contracted providers solely to operate and document this program, never for their independent marketing. Text messaging remains paused while sender registration and operational review are completed.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry, service, consent, and suppression records as needed to handle requests, operate services, document permissions, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion; some records may need to be retained for these purposes. We use access controls and server-side processing; no online service can guarantee complete security.

Your choices

You can submit either form without SMS consent, change analytics preferences, clear browser storage, decline optional external tools, and contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of a recorded SMS preference. We may verify your identity before acting. When messaging is active, reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Available privacy rights depend on applicable law.

Children, processing, and changes

This site is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13. Providers may process information in the United States or other countries. We update this notice when practices change and request new consent when required.
